NATURAL STONE · JUJUY, ARGENTINA

StoniqueS.A.U.

Excellence in social, artisanal and productive mining from the heart of the Argentine Northwest.

Enter

Institutional

Stonique S.A.U. connects production, territorial identity and responsible development to bring Jujuy's minerals to market.

Mission

To represent, support and promote Jujuy's artisanal social-mining producers by advancing formalization, safe work, value added at origin and direct trade.

Vision

To be a leading company in Argentina's Northwest, recognized for advancing sustainable, innovative and competitive mining rooted in local communities and connected to national and international markets.

Objectives

  • Raise the profile of Jujuy's mineral production.
  • Promote training and occupational safety.
  • Connect producers with new markets.
  • Protect natural and cultural heritage.
Our pillars
✦ Sustainable development✦ Value added at origin✦ Formalization and safety✦ Direct international trade
